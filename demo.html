Not a real drive — a simulation. Starts parked at the track's start/finish point, ramps up to a constant 100 km/h for the entire lap (there's no way to actually hold that everywhere on the Nordschleife — sharp corners like the Karussell would need real braking — this is deliberately unrealistic on speed so you can see the whole layout, HUD and Forward view at one steady pace), then ramps back down and parks again, looping until you hit Stop. Speed is fixed and synthetic; lateral G is estimated from the real track's curvature at that constant speed (smoothed over ~40m, clamped to ±3g) — so corners really do read as harder or softer relative to each other, it's just not measured from an accelerometer. No elevation data for this route, so vertical G reads 0. Publishes to the same MQTT channel/schema as the real logger, so the viewer can't tell the difference — open it in another tab/device to watch.
