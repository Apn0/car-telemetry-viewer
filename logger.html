Runs entirely in this tab — no app to install. Needs GPS + motion-sensor permission (iOS asks once per session when you tap Start), and the screen has to stay on with this tab in the foreground the whole time you're logging: browsers don't get background GPS the way a native app does, so switching apps or letting the screen lock pauses logging. Data streams live to the same public HiveMQ broker as the Android app — anyone with your channel ID can watch it in the viewer. Samples are also buffered here for Download CSV, but that buffer only lives in this tab, so download it before you close the page.
