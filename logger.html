Logging starts on its own when this page opens and every sample is written to this device immediately, so nothing is lost if you close the tab, reload, lose signal or the browser crashes — the run is picked up again next time you open the page. Samples upload to cloud storage in the background whenever there's a connection, and anything queued while you're out of coverage is sent once you're back.
